A short midday meditation for a busy mind

Between one task and the next, stay seated wherever you already are. Give each of these three steps about a minute.

3 minutes · At your desk or in a chair

Listen to the guided step ↓

Try a three-minute seated pause

  1. Notice a point of contact

    Rest your hands on your legs or the desk. Feel the surface under your palms or the soles of your feet. Keep your eyes open if that feels more comfortable.

  2. Come back when your mind wanders

    Give yourself a minute to notice where your attention keeps going. Start with whichever of these feels more familiar.

    What’s on your mind between meetings?

    If you keep replaying a meeting

    Feel the weight of your heels. Let that conversation wait for one breath. Feel the floor still there.

    Get check-in ↓

    If your eyes and shoulders are tired from the screen

    Look past the screen for one quiet color. Let your eyes rest there while your shoulders settle.

    Get check-in ↓

  3. Decide how to continue

    Notice the room and choose one small action to take next. That could be standing up, taking a sip of water, or opening the first document you need.

Adapt the practice to where you are

In a shared workspace, look at one spot and quietly notice your feet on the floor. If you have only a minute, stay with that point of contact until you’re ready to move on.

If you would rather listen, choose one steady sound, like a fan or traffic in the distance. Notice when your attention shifts and return to that sound.

Set the sound for your workspace

If you choose a guided session, headphones can be helpful in a shared room. In the player, open the volume controls. Lower Music volume until you can follow the spoken cues, then tap Apply.

Replaying a meeting

I’m sitting at my desk replaying something I said in a meeting. Please guide me through a three-minute pause now. Keep my eyes open and bring my attention back to my heels on the floor whenever the conversation comes to mind. End by helping me choose one small thing to do next.

Tired from screens

I’ve been looking at a screen all morning and my shoulders feel tight. I have three minutes at my desk. Please guide me through a meditation now with my eyes open. Start with a color across the room, and leave a little quiet to let my eyes rest and my shoulders settle.
