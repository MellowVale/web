Frequently asked questions

How is the meditation personalized?

You start with a short chat. Mellow Vale uses what you share about your mood or day to prepare a guided audio session.

Can I try Mellow Vale for free?

Yes. Guests can create up to 7 meditations in a rolling 7-day period. A free account raises that to 14. Mellow Vale Plus removes the weekly limit. See monthly and annual prices in the app.

Do I need an account?

You can start as a guest. Sessions you create while signed in stay linked to your account across devices.

How long is a session?

Guided sessions are short, and the length varies. The player shows how much time is left.

Can I change the sound?

Yes. Preview voices here, then choose your narrator in the app. In the player you can change playback speed and adjust the overall and music volumes separately. Play voice samples.

Which meditation languages are available?

The current catalogue has 155 language and regional choices with 980 voice options. Each language has its own voices; US English currently has 155. These choices are for narration. The app’s menus are in English. Browse all languages and voices.

Can I keep my sessions secular?

Yes. None (Secular) is the default. You can choose a faith tradition in Spiritual mode or return to secular sessions whenever you like. See how Spiritual mode works.
