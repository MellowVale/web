Frequently asked questions

How is the meditation personalized?

You start with a short chat. Mellow Vale uses what you share about your mood or day to prepare a guided audio session.

Can I try Mellow Vale for free?

Yes. Guests can create up to 7 meditations in a rolling 7-day period. A free account raises that to 14. Mellow Vale Plus removes the weekly limit. See monthly and annual prices in the app.

Do I need an account?

You can start as a guest. Sessions you create while signed in stay linked to your account across devices.

How long is a session?

Guided sessions are short, and the length varies. The player shows how much time is left.

Can I change the sound?

Yes. Choose a narration voice in Settings. In the player you can change playback speed and adjust overall volume and background music level separately.

Which meditation languages are available?

The current catalogue has 154 language and regional choices with 714 voice options. Each language has its own voices; US English currently has 65. These choices are for narration. The app’s menus are in English. Browse all languages and voices.

Can I keep my sessions secular?

Yes. The default is None (Secular). If you choose an optional spiritual mode, your meditation can include readings or mantras that reflect your selection.

How are my check-ins used?

Your check-in is processed online to create your guided session. Read how Mellow Vale handles app data.
