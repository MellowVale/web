Choose the sound of your session

  1. Set your meditation language

    Open Settings → Language. Choose the language and regional option you want, then tap Update.

  2. Preview and choose a voice

    Open Settings → Voice. Use the play buttons to hear samples, tap the voice you prefer, then tap Update.

  3. Open a session from your chat

    Return to your chat and open a meditation. Your selected language and voice are used when preparing the audio. Stay connected while it is prepared.

Make the guidance comfortable to follow

Try a voice sample before choosing. Once you open the meditation, adjust playback speed and music volume so the spoken guidance feels comfortable to follow.

You can choose a different language or voice before opening a session from your chat. Changing a setting does not switch the voice in the middle of audio that is already playing.
