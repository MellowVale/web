A five-minute meditation to unwind after work

Use this practice right after your last task, once you’re somewhere you can pause safely. Put your work screen away, keep a place to jot down one reminder, and give yourself one minute for each step.

5 minutes · Where you put down your work things

Listen or copy a check-in ↓

Make the transition in five minutes

  1. Mark the end of work

    Close the laptop, take off your badge, or put your work bag down. Pause for the rest of the minute and notice what your hands feel like once the task is over.

  2. Leave yourself one reminder

    If an unfinished task keeps coming back, write one line with the first action for next time, such as “Check the numbers before sending the draft.” Put the note where you’ll see it when you return.

  3. Notice what you’re still carrying

    Give what’s on your mind a word or two, like “frustration,” “relief,” or “still switched on.” Notice where that feeling shows up. Let your hands rest wherever they’re comfortable.

  4. Look and listen around you

    Let your eyes settle on one nearby object and notice its shape or color. Listen for one close sound and one farther away. If a work thought returns, bring your attention back to what you can see or hear.

  5. Begin the rest of your day

    Picture the first thing you’d like to do now, whether it’s changing clothes, saying hello to someone, or stepping outside. Pause for one natural breath, then take that first step.

Find a cue that fits the way you work

If you work from home, leave your desk and do the practice in another chair. If you work away from home, try it when you arrive and put your keys down. This works after a morning or night shift, too.

If you drive home, wait until you’ve parked and finished driving. If you have only a minute, close with the reminder in step two and notice one thing around you before you move on.
